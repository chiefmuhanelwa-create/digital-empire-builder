Module 02
"Where Are You Now?"
The Knowledge Entrepreneur Scorecard

Score yourself honestly across the 5 axes below, 0–10.

Score each axis, 0 (not started) to 10 (fully built)
Audience — do you have people who actually pay attention when you speak? ___ / 10
Authority — would a stranger believe you're credible in your area within 30 seconds? ___ / 10
Offer — do you have something specific someone could pay you for today? ___ / 10
Systems — could this run for a week without you actively managing it? ___ / 10
Finance — do you know exactly what you'd charge, and why? ___ / 10
Your lowest score above is your real starting point — not the one that feels most urgent. What's your lowest-scoring axis?
This score isn't a grade — it's a map. Whichever axis is lowest is where the next real step lives, not wherever feels most exciting to work on.

Module 03
Called Expert Method — P³
The Positioning Blueprint

Three questions. One sentence at the end.

Who do you help? (be specific — not "creators," but "nurses," "small accounting firm owners," "youth pastors")
What do you help them do? (the outcome, not the method)
Why should they listen to you, specifically — not someone else?
Now combine your three answers into one sentence
"I help ______________________ achieve ______________________ through ______________________."
Example
"I help nurses turn clinical knowledge into income-producing digital assets."

The 4E Framework
Your First Content Engine

Every piece of content does one of four jobs. Most people only ever do one of them.

The 4 content jobs
Educate — teach something real. Your frameworks, your process, your point of view.
Entertain — humanize you. Behind the scenes, stories, personality.
Encourage — proof it works. Wins, transformations, testimonials — yours or your audience's.
Earn — a direct, specific, respectful ask.

Module 05
Audience Ownership
The River → Fish → Tank Framework

Followers you don't own can disappear overnight. This page starts the move to what you do own.

The three parts
River — the rented platform (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). People flow past. You don't own the water.
Fish — the people who stop and pay attention. Still not yours until they leave the river.
Tank — your owned list (email, WhatsApp community). This is the only place a platform can't touch.
Which platform is your "river" right now?
What's one specific, useful thing you could offer in exchange for an email address this week? (your first lead magnet — one sentence is enough for now)
