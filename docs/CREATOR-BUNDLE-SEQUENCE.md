> exists. This is the full teaching arc, moving loosely in the direction of the real,
> canonical Kingdom Contentpreneur Transformation System:
>
> **Knowledge/Experience/Story → Content Strategy → Audience → Community → Assets →
> Impact → Legacy**
>
> **Rebuilt 2026-08-05 per direct correction:** the 7-stage system is a compass for
> the *arc*, not a checklist each email has to visibly teach. Earlier drafts read like
> a framework lecture — each email announcing its stage and walking through it like a
> syllabus. That's gone. Every email now leads by naming a real pain point the reader
> is actually sitting in, right now, in plain language — making it clear *I've been
> there too, I'm aware of exactly what this feels like* — before the personal story
> ever starts. The story proves the pain point was real. The insight after it is the
> hope: something true and useful, not just a lesson label. Every email is judged by
> one question: does this actually give the reader something, or is it just moving
> them toward the next email? Asks were cut hard — most emails now have at most one
> soft, optional invitation to reply, not stacked homework ("did you open the
> workbook AND reply with X AND tell me Y"). Give first.
>
> Checked against real signal: Unathi Mabunda ("I am not starting from zero, but I
> need direction") is the right-fit reader this whole sequence is written for. Each
> email still points toward the HOW (a small guide, Foundation Kit, or Accelerator)
> where it's earned, per the Constitution's own stated split: *"Content sells WHAT and
> WHY... the Accelerator sells HOW."* — but the CTA is never the reason the email
> exists.
>
> **Voice rules applied throughout:** present-tense scenes, not case studies. A real
> reflection question in every email, set apart, meant to actually be sat with — not
> a rhetorical throwaway. Short declarative sentences. `{$name}` woven into the
> thinking, not just the greeting. Faith woven in naturally, after the practical
> lesson lands, never before it, never as a citation-dropping exercise — allusion over
> chapter-and-verse except where a verse is already verified (Proverbs 13:22, Email
> 8). No `{$name|there}` fallback, per direct instruction — uses plain `{$name}`.

**Send cadence:** every 2–3 days after Email 1. **Subjects ~20 characters**, mixing
question and name per MailerLite's own advice.
